# Standing rules

This repo is public and built in public. Keep it up to date as you work.

- When a roadmap checkbox's gate passes, tick it and link the evidence.
- At each phase close, write a milestone report in `reports/phase-N.md` covering: what was done, issues faced and how they were corrected, what it means for building Modus, and what was verified versus not.
- Never mark anything Proven before its gate passes.
- Never push without asking me first.
- Scan for secrets, personal paths and account details before every push.
- No Co-Authored-By or Claude-Session lines in commits or PRs.
